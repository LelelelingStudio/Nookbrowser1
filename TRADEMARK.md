## What you may do without asking

- Fork, modify, build and distribute the code under a different name.
- Say truthfully that your work is derived from Lelele Browser.
- Refer to Lelele Browser by name in discussion, documentation, reviews and comparisons.
- Build and run your own copy under any name you like.

## What needs permission

- Distributing a modified version under the name "Lelele Browser" or a confusingly similar name.
- Using the Lelele Browser wordmark or app icon on your fork, its listing, website or marketing.
- Naming a product, service or company in a way that suggests it is Lelele Browser or endorsed by Leleleling Studio.

Rename your fork and use your own icon. That is the whole requirement.

## Why

GPL-3.0 permits selling copies and modifications. What the license cannot stop is someone shipping a rebranded copy under Lelele Browser's own name, so that its bugs, data handling and support burden land on this project.
